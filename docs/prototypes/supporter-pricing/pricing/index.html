Do I need to pay to use Yapcam?

No. Recording, editing and local export stay available without supporting the project. This supporter concept adds no paid recording limits, watermarks or feature gates.

Am I buying an actual webcam?

No. Monkey Cam is a proposed cosmetic frame for your webcam overlay inside Yapcam. It is not hardware, a camera driver, improved camera quality or a new recording feature. This page only previews the idea; the frame is not integrated into the app yet.

What happens when I cancel?

In this proposal, monthly and annual support renew automatically until cancelled. Cancelling stops the next renewal. The cosmetic frame stays yours after your first successful payment, and the free app is unchanged. There is no active subscription on this prototype.

How does yearly support work?

The example annual price is €30, charged once per year and renewing annually. That's €2.50 per month in equivalent cost, not monthly billing. Twelve €3 monthly payments would total €36, so the proposed annual option saves €6.

Will this page charge me anything?

No. This is an interactive design prototype. It has no checkout, payment provider, account system, analytics or supporter database. The thank-you and banana count are local demos that reset when you reload.
